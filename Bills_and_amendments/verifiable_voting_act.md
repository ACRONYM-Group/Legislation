PROPOSITION IN COUNCIL:

The following is proposed to the Council of Nomes. In Council, the proposed change will be an amendment to the Constitution of Nerdiness.

**Preamble**

The following proposed changes include:

Replacement of the ballot-relative ranked-choice scoring formula in Article 1 Section 2 Subsection 1 with an independent per-candidate scoring system, removing the ambiguity created when voters rank differing numbers of candidates.

Formal recognition of virtual and asynchronous participation in meetings and elections, so that quorum, voting, and official action no longer require simultaneous physical presence.

---

**Article 1; Meetings**

**Article 1 Section 1; When**

A meeting of A.C.R.O.N.Y.M. is held at least once a month tentatively scheduled for the second Saturday of every month, or when a member of the council of Nomes calls one. A lack of a quorum of at least half of the Nymphs and two of the Nomes prevents official action from being taken at a meeting. Each meeting is labeled by the meeting number and the session number. Sessions start on the second Saturday of July, starting with Session 0 starting on July 16th 2016. A new session marks an election for president.

<u>Article 1 Section 1 Subsection 1; Virtual Participation</u>

<u>A meeting of A.C.R.O.N.Y.M. need not occur at a single physical location. Any member may participate in a meeting, cast a vote, or be counted toward quorum remotely, provided they do so through the means designated by the Office of the President or, for elections specifically, through the group's designated voting system.</u>

<u>Where official action requires a vote, the President (or, for a presidential election, the outgoing President or a delegated Nome) shall announce a Voting Window — a defined start and end time during which votes may be cast. A member's participation is counted toward quorum if their vote or attendance is recorded at any point within the Voting Window, regardless of whether they were ever simultaneously present with other members. A Voting Window for a monthly meeting shall be no shorter than 24 hours; a Voting Window for a presidential or Council election shall be no shorter than 72 hours, to give reasonable opportunity for all members to participate.</u>

**Article 1 Section 2; What**

Elections for the next President of ACRONYM will occur on the first meeting of a session or the first meeting held with a quorum of at least half of the voting body present. The election will start with a vote of all members of ACRONYM via rank choice voting. The top three candidates are selected as the members of the Council of Nomes.

Once the Council of Nomes has been selected there is a second rank choice vote among the members of the Council of Nomes, the top candidate in this vote will be the President of ACRONYM for the next Session.

**Article 1 Section 2 Subsection 1; Vote Counting**

<u>Each voter shall independently assign every candidate a score from 0 to 5, where 0 indicates no support and 5 indicates strongest support. A voter is not required to assign every candidate a distinct score, nor is a voter required to score every candidate, any candidate left unscored is treated as having been given a score of 0. A candidate's total is the sum of the scores given by all voters who cast a ballot in that election. The candidate(s) with the highest total, per Article 1 Section 2, are elected.</u>

**Article 1 Section 2 Subsection 2; Supervision**

The counting of the votes for any element of the election of the President of ACRONYM or the council of nomes requires there be one member of the Nymphery and one Nome to each separately count the ballots. If there is a disagreement regarding the final count, two other members will be delegated to perform a recount.

<u>Where votes are counted by A.C.R.O.N.Y.M.'s designated cryptographic voting system, the Nymph and Nome required by this Subsection shall each independently run the system's public verification process against the published results, in place of a manual count. A "disagreement," for purposes of this Subsection, means the verification process reports a failure for one auditor but not the other; the two additional members delegated to recount shall likewise each independently run the verification process.</u>

**Article 1 Section 2 Subsection 3; Tiebreakers**

If during the course of the election for the council of Nomes, there is a tie which results in there not being a clear top three, those who would be in the Council and those involved in the tie will be put on a runoff ballot to determine the top three. This second vote shall follow the same rules and processes as the first.

If during the course of the presidential election, there is a tie that results in there not being a clear top candidate, then a second vote shall be conducted among the top two candidates. In the event that there is a three way tie during the presidential election, the candidate who totalled the least number of votes during the Council election shall be disqualified.
